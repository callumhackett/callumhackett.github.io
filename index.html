Callum Hackett

I’m a researcher in human and artificial intelligence, particularly interested in the relationship between language and knowledge. Drawing on computer science, linguistics and philosophy, I support the view that intelligence is embodied and that AI is a cultural technology that does not think or feel.

Writing

I write about AI and philosophy at Wrong Timeline, where I explore the idea that efforts to reverse-engineer human intelligence took a wrong turn historically when monied interests favoured rationalism over cybernetics.

I’m also drafting a book on pseudoscience in the AI industry and have published academic work on the biological origins of language.

In parallel, I’m a classical pianist and editor of Leitmotiv, a music review featuring multi-media essays about the art of attentive listening.
